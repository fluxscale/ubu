# ubu Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

ubu is a menu bar utility from Fluxscale Limited that keeps your Dock on your main display.

## What ubu collects

Nothing. ubu has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking, and it sends no personal data anywhere.

## What ubu does on your Mac

- **Pointer position.** With the Accessibility permission you grant, ubu watches pointer movement to tell when the pointer is pressing against the edge of a secondary display. It can move the pointer a few points away from that edge, or briefly push it against your main display's edge to bring the Dock back. Pointer positions are only used in the moment and are never stored or sent.
- **Display layout and Dock location.** ubu reads your display arrangement and the Dock's window position from macOS. This information never leaves your Mac.
- **Settings.** ubu saves its own settings, such as whether it has registered as a login item, in its local preferences.

## Network

- **App Store edition:** purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple through StoreKit. ubu makes no network requests of its own. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **GitHub edition:** ubu asks the GitHub Releases API (`api.github.com`) for the latest version number when it launches and when you choose Check for Updates. That request contains no personal data. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

ubu collects no data from anyone, including children.
